Job Description: Senior Java Developer 
About the Role 
We’re looking for a Senior Java Developer who can hit the ground running and bring top-notch coding expertise to our team. 
As a senior member, you’ll not only write clean, efficient, and maintainable code but also mentor junior developers, 
contribute to architectural decisions, and ensure our applications run like a well-oiled machine. 

Responsibilities:
Design, develop, and maintain high-performance Java applications using Spring Boot, 
Microservices, and REST APIs. Collaborate with cross-functional teams (Product, DevOps, QA) to deliver end-to-end solutions. 
Implement cloud-native applications leveraging Azure/AWS/GCP. 
Optimize application performance and scalability — making sure our systems don’t just run, but fly. 
Participate in code reviews and enforce best practices for secure, efficient coding. 
Troubleshoot production issues, rolling up your sleeves when the going gets tough.
Mentor and guide junior developers, passing the torch of best practices. 

Required Skills & Experience:
8 years of professional experience in Java development. 
Strong knowledge of Java 11/17/21 and solid understanding of OOP principles. 
Hands-on expertise with Spring Boot, Hibernate/JPA, Microservices architecture. 
Proficiency in SQL/NoSQL databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL, Cosmos DB, MongoDB). 
Experience with cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, or GCP). 
Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines, Docker, and Kubernetes. 
Familiarity with messaging systems (Kafka, RabbitMQ, or Azure Service Bus). 
Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to think on your feet. Strong communication and teamwork skills. 

Nice-to-Have:
Experience with GraphQL or Reactive Programming. 
Exposure to big data pipelines or event-driven systems. 
Knowledge of front-end frameworks (React, Angular) for full-stack development.
